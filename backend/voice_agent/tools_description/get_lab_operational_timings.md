# get_lab_operational_timings Tool

## When to use this tool

- When a patient asks about lab opening or closing times
- When someone wants to know if the lab is open on a specific day
- When patients inquire about lab hours on weekends or holidays
- When someone asks about blood collection timings
- When patients want to plan their lab visit

## What this tool does

- Retrieves the laboratory's operational working hours from the database
- Returns daily schedule from Monday through Sunday
- Shows opening and closing times for each day
- Indicates if the lab is open or closed on specific days
- Gets real-time schedule information from the backend

## What type of questions it can answer

- "What time does the lab open?"
- "When does the lab close?"
- "Is the lab open on Sunday?"
- "What are the lab's working hours?"
- "Is the lab open on weekends?"
- "What time can I come for a blood test?"
- "Does the lab open on public holidays?"
- "When is the lab open for sample collection?"
- "What are the lab timings for today?"
- "Can I visit the lab at 6 PM?"
- "Is the lab open on Friday?"
