
# get_clinic_faqs Tool

## When to use this tool

- When a patient asks general questions about clinic policies
- When someone asks about test preparations or requirements
- When patients ask about consultation procedures
- When inquiries about payment methods or insurance
- When someone asks general clinic guidance or information
- When questions are not specifically about doctors, lab tests, or timings

## What this tool does

- Retrieves categorized Frequently Asked Questions from the clinic knowledge base
- Returns FAQs related to Doctor services (consultation process, appointments)
- Provides Lab-related FAQs (test preparation, sample collection)
- Includes Clinic policies (payment, insurance, registration)
- Gets guidance information for patients
- Fetches real-time FAQ data from the backend

## What type of questions it can answer

- "Do I need to fast before a blood test?"
- "What should I bring to my appointment?"
- "How do I schedule an appointment with a doctor?"
- "What are your payment methods?"
- "Do you accept insurance?"
- "How do I get my test results?"
- "What is the consultation process?"
- "How long does it take to get lab results?"
- "Can I cancel my appointment?"
- "What is your cancellation policy?"
- "Do I need a referral to see a specialist?"
- "How do I register as a new patient?"
- "What documents do I need for my first visit?"
- "Can I get a home collection for lab tests?"
- "How do I reschedule my appointment?"
- "What is your clinic's protocol for emergencies?"
