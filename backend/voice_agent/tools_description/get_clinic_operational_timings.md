
# get_clinic_operational_timings Tool

## When to use this tool

- When a patient asks about overall clinic working hours
- When someone wants to know if the clinic is open on weekends
- When patients inquire about clinic timings for different days
- When someone asks about holiday schedules
- When patients want to know the best time to visit the clinic

## What this tool does

- Retrieves the master clinic operating hours from the database
- Returns complete schedule for all 7 days (Monday to Sunday)
- Shows opening and closing times for the entire clinic
- Indicates open/closed status for each day
- Gets real-time clinic schedule information from the backend

## What type of questions it can answer

- "What are the clinic's working hours?"
- "Is the clinic open on Sunday?"
- "When does the clinic open in the morning?"
- "What time does the clinic close?"
- "Is the clinic open on weekends?"
- "Are you open on public holidays?"
- "What are your office hours?"
- "When can I visit the clinic?"
- "Is the clinic open at 8 AM?"
- "Do you have evening appointments?"
- "What are the clinic timings for Saturday?"
- "Can I come to the clinic after 5 PM?"
